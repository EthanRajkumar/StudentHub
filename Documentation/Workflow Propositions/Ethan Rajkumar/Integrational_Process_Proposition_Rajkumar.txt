Among many framework UI's including Windows Forms, SCiter stands out as a modern HTML/CSS embedder framework, allowing web-development to coexist with a C++ backend. Using HTML/CSS would complicate the tech stack, but makes creating responsive UI much easier than its code-based counterparts.  

https://sciter.com/
